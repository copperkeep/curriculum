- `lru_cache` / `cache` — the memoize you just wrote, as a decorator
- `partial(f, x)` — a new function with some arguments pre-filled
- `reduce(f, xs, start)` — fold a sequence into one value
- `wraps` — used when writing decorators (next module)
- `total_ordering` — fill in `<=`, `>`, `>=` from `__eq__` and `__lt__`

`reduce` is rarely clearer than a loop or `sum`/`max`; `partial` and `cache` earn
their place often.
