Build the core of a lending library:

- `Book` — a frozen dataclass: `isbn`, `title`, `author`
- `Library` — add books, lend them to members, return them
- `LendingError` — raised for impossible actions (lending a book that is out, or
  not in the catalogue)
- `overdue()` — a generator of loans past their due date
- `to_json()` / `from_json()` — save and restore the whole library

Each step builds on the last, and each is graded on its own. Design the pieces
so they can be tested alone — that is the real lesson.
